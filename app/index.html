Every Black Friday price below was re-checked this morning and scored 0–100 — the strongest deals sweep to the top.

How the score works: discount % + verdict bonus (new low +22, strong +10, routine 0, inflated −18) + up to 12 for dollars saved. Every input is printed on the card, so the score can be recomputed by hand.

Every price was re-read on Saturday 28 November 2026 in a source that names the store and the price; the check time and source sit on every card. Where a source measures a discount against a comparable value or a stated previous price, the evidence says so. Prices move during the event — the store's page at checkout is the final word.
